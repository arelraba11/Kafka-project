Overview:
The Tesla Model 3 Long Range is one of the world's best-selling electric vehicles, combining impressive range, advanced driver assistance, and over-the-air software updates that improve the car after purchase. It is a mid-size all-electric sedan and Tesla's most affordable long-range vehicle.

Range and Performance:
- EPA-estimated range: 358 miles (576 km) on a single charge
- 0 to 60 mph (0–97 km/h) in 4.2 seconds
- 0 to 100 km/h in 4.4 seconds
- Top speed: 145 mph (233 km/h)
- Dual motor all-wheel drive (front + rear motors)
- Regenerative braking captures energy during deceleration

Battery and Charging:
- Long Range lithium-iron-phosphate (LFP) battery pack
- Battery retains at least 70% capacity after 150,000 miles (240,000 km)
- Tesla Supercharger network: over 50,000 connectors in 50+ countries
- Peak charging rate: up to 250 kW at V3 Supercharger
- 10% to 80% charge in approximately 25 minutes at V3 Supercharger
- Home charging (11 kW AC): full charge overnight (~8 hours)
- Compatible with all public AC chargers (J1772 adapter included)

Autopilot and Safety:
- Standard Autopilot included: adaptive cruise control, lane centering, auto lane change
- Traffic-Aware Cruise Control and Autosteer on divided highways
- Full Self-Driving (FSD) package: Navigate on Autopilot, Smart Summon, Autopark — available as paid upgrade (~30,000 ILS / ~8,000 USD)
- Hardware: 8 cameras (360-degree vision), ultrasonic sensors
- Automatic Emergency Braking, Blind Spot Monitoring, Side Collision Warning
- NHTSA 5-star safety rating in all categories
- Euro NCAP 5-star safety rating

Who Should Buy the Tesla Model 3:
The Tesla Model 3 is ideal for drivers who want to eliminate fuel costs, reduce environmental impact, and benefit from cutting-edge software and autonomous driving technology. It is particularly cost-effective for high-mileage drivers in countries with cheap electricity.

Recommended for:
- Daily commuters (long range eliminates range anxiety)
- Tech-forward drivers who enjoy software updates and automation
- Environmentally conscious buyers
- Drivers in cities with good Supercharger coverage

Not recommended for:
- Buyers in countries with limited charging infrastructure
- Very long rural road trips without Supercharger stations nearby
- Budget buyers (entry-level options from other brands available below 100,000 ILS)

Climate and Buying Considerations:
- The car operates in temperatures from -30°C to +50°C, but range decreases significantly in extreme cold (below 0°C, expect 20–30% range reduction).
- In hot climates (above 35°C), the battery thermal management system activates, which may use extra energy but protects battery longevity.
- In cities with temperatures regularly above 30°C (e.g., São Paulo, Brazil in summer), the car's air conditioning and battery cooling add extra energy draw. Consider scheduling charging during off-peak electricity hours.
- For buyers in São Paulo: temperatures in late September typically range from 15°C to 28°C — usually below the 30°C threshold, making it a comfortable season to purchase and use the vehicle.
