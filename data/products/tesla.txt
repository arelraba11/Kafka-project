Product: Tesla Model 3 (Long Range)
Category: Electric Vehicle
Brand: Tesla
Price: 370000 ILS (approximately 100000 USD)

The Tesla Model 3 Long Range is one of the world's best-selling electric vehicles, combining impressive range, advanced technology, and over-the-air software updates. It is a mid-size sedan manufactured by Tesla, headquartered in Austin, Texas.

Range and Performance:
- EPA-estimated range: 358 miles (576 km) on a single charge
- 0 to 60 mph in 4.2 seconds (Long Range All-Wheel Drive)
- Top speed: 145 mph
- Dual motor all-wheel drive

Battery and Charging:
- Lithium-ion battery pack, retains 70% capacity after 150,000 miles
- Supercharger network: over 50,000 connectors worldwide
- Up to 250 kW peak charging at V3 Supercharger
- Charges from 10% to 80% in approximately 25 minutes

Autopilot and Safety:
- Standard Autopilot: adaptive cruise control, lane centering, automatic emergency braking
- Full Self-Driving (FSD) package available as optional upgrade
- 8 cameras, ultrasonic sensors, and forward-facing radar
- NHTSA 5-star safety rating in all categories

Interior and Technology:
- 15.4-inch central touchscreen controlling all vehicle functions
- Over-the-air software updates for new features and improvements
- Premium audio system with 14 speakers
- Glass roof with UV and infrared protection
- Wireless phone charging

Ecosystem:
- Tesla mobile app for remote monitoring, climate control, and charging
- Integration with home solar and Powerwall energy storage systems
- Available colors: Pearl White, Midnight Silver, Deep Blue, Solid Black, Red

Comparison:
- The Tesla is significantly more expensive than the iPhone (370000 ILS vs 3700 ILS) and the MacBook (370000 ILS vs 7400 ILS).
- The iPhone costs approximately 1% of the Tesla price.
- The MacBook costs approximately 2% of the Tesla price.
- You could buy approximately 100 iPhones or 50 MacBooks for the price of one Tesla Model 3.
