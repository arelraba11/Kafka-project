Tesla is an American electric vehicle and clean energy company founded in 2003 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. Tesla designs and manufactures battery electric vehicles, energy storage systems, and solar products. The company's vehicle lineup includes the Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y, Cybertruck, and the Semi truck.

The Tesla Model 3 is one of the best-selling electric vehicles in the world. It offers a Long Range variant with an EPA-estimated range of over 350 miles on a single charge. The Model 3 features over-the-air software updates that can add new features, improve performance, and enhance safety without requiring a visit to a service center.

Tesla vehicles use Autopilot as a standard driver assistance system, which includes features like adaptive cruise control, lane centering, and automatic emergency braking. The Full Self-Driving package adds additional capabilities such as Navigate on Autopilot, automatic lane changes, and traffic and stop sign control. Tesla uses a large fleet of vehicles to collect data that continually trains its neural network-based driving system.

Tesla Supercharger is a global network of fast-charging stations designed exclusively for Tesla vehicles, with over 50,000 connectors worldwide as of 2024. The Model Y is the best-selling vehicle globally and comes in Standard Range, Long Range, and Performance trims. Tesla batteries use lithium-ion chemistry and are designed to retain at least 70 percent of capacity after 150,000 miles of driving.
