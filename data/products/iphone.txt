Overview:
The iPhone 15 Pro is Apple's flagship smartphone. It features a lightweight titanium frame — the first iPhone to use aerospace-grade titanium — and a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with ProMotion 120Hz adaptive refresh rate. It is powered by the A17 Pro chip, built on a 3-nanometer process, delivering industry-leading CPU and GPU performance.

Processor and Performance:
- Apple A17 Pro chip (3nm process)
- 6-core CPU (2 performance + 4 efficiency cores)
- 6-core GPU with hardware ray tracing
- 16-core Neural Engine for on-device AI

Camera System:
- 48MP main camera with second-generation sensor-shift OIS and 24mm focal length
- 12MP ultrawide camera with autofocus and macro photography
- 12MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom (77mm focal length)
- Supports 4K ProRes video recording at 60fps
- Photonic Engine and Smart HDR 5 for computational photography
