Product: iPhone 15 Pro
Category: Smartphone
Brand: Apple
Price: 3700 ILS (approximately 1000 USD)

The iPhone 15 Pro is Apple's flagship smartphone, featuring a lightweight titanium frame and a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with ProMotion 120Hz adaptive refresh rate. It is powered by the A17 Pro chip, built on a 3-nanometer process, delivering industry-leading CPU and GPU performance.

Camera System:
- 48MP main camera with second-generation sensor-shift OIS
- 12MP ultrawide camera with autofocus
- 12MP telephoto camera with 3x optical zoom
- Supports 4K ProRes video recording

Key Features:
- Face ID biometric authentication
- USB-C connector (replaces Lightning)
- Apple Pay contactless payments
- Satellite connectivity for emergency SOS
- iOS 17 with ongoing software updates

Battery and Connectivity:
- Up to 23 hours video playback
- MagSafe wireless charging
- 5G connectivity
- Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.3

Ecosystem Integration:
- Seamless sync with Mac, iPad, Apple Watch via iCloud
- AirDrop, Handoff, and Universal Clipboard support
- Available storage: 128GB, 256GB, 512GB, 1TB

The iPhone is ideal for users who prioritize camera quality, ecosystem integration, and long software support. It is a premium product positioned at the top of the smartphone market.
