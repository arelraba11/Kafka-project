Overview:
The MacBook Pro 14-inch with M3 Pro is Apple's professional-grade laptop, designed for users who need sustained high performance for demanding workloads such as software development, video editing, 3D rendering, and data science. It features a Liquid Retina XDR display with ProMotion technology.

Processor and Performance:
- Apple M3 Pro chip (12-core CPU: 6 performance + 6 efficiency; 18-core GPU)
- Up to 36GB unified memory (LPDDR5, shared CPU/GPU pool)
- Up to 512GB SSD storage (configurable up to 4TB)
- Hardware-accelerated ray tracing, mesh shading, and MetalFX upscaling
- 40 TOPS Neural Engine for on-device machine learning

Display:
- 14.2-inch Liquid Retina XDR (mini-LED)
- 3024 x 1964 resolution at 254 ppi
- ProMotion 120Hz adaptive refresh (1Hz–120Hz)
- 1000 nits sustained brightness, 1600 nits peak HDR
- P3 wide color gamut, True Tone, 1,000,000:1 contrast ratio

Battery and Charging:
- Up to 18 hours battery life (Apple's longest-lasting MacBook Pro ever)
- 96W USB-C power adapter included
- MagSafe 3 magnetic charging (fast charge: 50% in 30 minutes)
- Charges via Thunderbolt 4 port as alternative

Ports and Connectivity:
- 3x Thunderbolt 4 / USB-C ports (40Gb/s, DisplayPort, power delivery)
- HDMI 2.1 (supports 8K displays)
- SDXC card reader (UHS-II, up to 312 MB/s)
- 3.5mm headphone jack with high-impedance headphone support
- Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax, 2.4/5/6GHz)
- Bluetooth 5.3

Audio and Camera:
- Six-speaker sound system with force-cancelling woofers and Spatial Audio
- 1080p FaceTime HD camera with Center Stage
- Studio-quality three-microphone array with directional beamforming

MacBook Air (M2) — Budget Alternative:
- Price: 5,400 ILS (~1,460 USD / ~1,350 EUR)
- Thinner, fanless design (passive cooling)
- Up to 18 hours battery life
- M2 chip (8-core CPU, 10-core GPU)
- Best for everyday tasks: web browsing, email, documents, light coding
- Not suitable for sustained heavy workloads (thermal throttling)

MacBook Pro vs MacBook Air:
- MacBook Pro: active cooling, ProMotion display, more ports, higher peak performance
- MacBook Air: lighter, quieter, cheaper, sufficient for most users

Who Should Buy the MacBook Pro:
Ideal for professionals who need a portable workstation. Software developers, video editors (Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve), 3D artists (Blender, Cinema 4D), machine learning engineers, and data scientists will see the most benefit.

Recommended for:
- Software developers and engineers
- Video and audio producers
- Data scientists and ML engineers
- Graphic designers and 3D artists

Not recommended for:
- Casual users (MacBook Air M2 is sufficient and costs less)
- Gaming-focused users (limited game library vs Windows)
- Budget-conscious buyers
