Product: MacBook Pro 14-inch (M3 Pro)
Category: Laptop Computer
Brand: Apple
Price: 7400 ILS (approximately 2000 USD)

The MacBook Pro 14-inch with M3 Pro is Apple's professional-grade laptop, designed for users who need sustained high performance for demanding workloads. It features a Liquid Retina XDR display with ProMotion technology, reaching up to 120Hz adaptive refresh rate and 1000 nits sustained brightness.

Processor and Performance:
- Apple M3 Pro chip (11-core CPU, 14-core GPU)
- Up to 36GB unified memory
- Up to 512GB SSD storage
- Hardware-accelerated ray tracing and mesh shading

Display:
- 14.2-inch Liquid Retina XDR
- 3024 x 1964 resolution (254 ppi)
- ProMotion 120Hz adaptive refresh
- 1000 nits sustained, 1600 nits peak HDR brightness

Battery and Charging:
- Up to 18 hours battery life
- MagSafe 3 magnetic charging
- Charges via Thunderbolt 4 as well

Ports and Connectivity:
- 3x Thunderbolt 4 (USB-C) ports
- HDMI 2.1
- SD card reader
- 3.5mm headphone jack
- Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.3

Audio and Camera:
- Six-speaker sound system with Spatial Audio
- 1080p FaceTime HD camera
- Studio-quality three-mic array

The MacBook Pro is best suited for software developers, video editors, 3D artists, and data scientists. Compared to the iPhone, it offers a full desktop-class experience with macOS. Compared to the Tesla, it is a personal productivity tool rather than a vehicle.

The MacBook Air (M2) is a lighter alternative at approximately 5400 ILS (1460 USD), suited for everyday tasks without the need for sustained peak performance.
