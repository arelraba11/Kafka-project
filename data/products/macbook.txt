The MacBook is a line of laptop computers designed and built by Apple Inc. The current MacBook lineup includes the MacBook Air and MacBook Pro, available in 13-inch, 14-inch, and 16-inch display sizes. All current MacBook models are powered by Apple Silicon chips, which replaced Intel processors starting in 2020.

The MacBook Air is Apple's thinnest and lightest laptop, weighing approximately 2.7 pounds for the 13-inch model. It uses the M2 or M3 chip depending on the configuration and features a fanless design, meaning it operates silently under most workloads. The MacBook Air is designed for everyday tasks including web browsing, document editing, media consumption, and lightweight creative work.

The MacBook Pro is designed for professional users who require sustained high performance. The 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro models use the M3 Pro or M3 Max chips, which deliver exceptional CPU and GPU performance for demanding workloads such as video editing, 3D rendering, and software compilation. The MacBook Pro includes a Liquid Retina XDR display with ProMotion technology and up to 120Hz refresh rate.

MacBook batteries are rated for up to 22 hours of video playback on the MacBook Pro 16-inch model. All MacBook models run macOS and support integration with other Apple devices through features like Handoff, Sidecar, and Universal Clipboard. MacBooks include a MagSafe charging connector, Thunderbolt ports, an HDMI port on the Pro models, and a built-in high-fidelity speaker system.
