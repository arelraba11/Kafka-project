## Resilience Scenario 1 — Worker Crash and Recovery

Failure: pkill -f mathApp  (math worker process terminated)

Recovery: bun run src/node/apps/mathApp.ts
          groupId=math-tool-worker → Consumer has joined the group

Query: what is 12 * 9
Conversation ID: 81413296-9482-4bc9-ada2-17a0c788cb42

Orchestrator: dispatching math → step complete  plan completed
Math worker:  12 * 9 = 108

Answer:
  The result of 12 multiplied by 9 is 108.

synthesizerLatency=1508ms

Explanation:
  Kafka's durable offset management guarantees no event is lost across a worker restart.
  The math-tool-worker consumer group committed its last offset before the crash.
  On restart, the consumer rejoined the group, rewound to the uncommitted offset,
  and processed the pending ToolInvocationRequested event.
  The Orchestrator's LevelDB state was intact throughout — no plan data was lost.

## Resilience Scenario 3 — Duplicate ToolInvocationRequested (Idempotency)

Setup: Manually produce a duplicate ToolInvocationRequested to tool-invocation-requests
       for a conversationId whose plan is already completed and deleted from LevelDB.

Conversation ID: resilience-orch-1774785939  (plan completed, deleted from LevelDB)

Duplicate event injected via kafka-console-producer:
  topic=tool-invocation-requests
  key=resilience-orch-1774785939
  value={"conversationId":"resilience-orch-1774785939","eventType":"ToolInvocationRequested",
         "payload":{"toolName":"exchange","input":{"currencyCode":"GBP","targetCurrency":"USD","amount":50}}}

Layer 1 — orchestrator-dedup consumer detects duplicate:
  [orchestrator] [dedup] conversationId=resilience-orch-1774785939
                         ToolInvocationRequested received but plan not found (already deleted)

Layer 2 — exchangeApp processes duplicate (idempotent — same input, same output):
  [exchange] tool conversationId=resilience-orch-1774785939 from=GBP to=USD amount=50
  → emits ToolInvocationResulted to conversation-events

Layer 3 — Orchestrator main consumer drops the duplicate result:
  [orchestrator] Received result for unknown conversationId=resilience-orch-1774785939, skipping.

Outcome: No double-answer delivered. No crash. No corrupted state.
         Both dedup layers absorbed the event cleanly.

Explanation:
  Workers are stateless and naturally idempotent — same input always produces same output.
  The Orchestrator's conversationId guard in onToolInvocationResulted() drops all results
  for plans no longer in LevelDB. The orchestrator-dedup consumer group provides an
  additional early-warning log for unexpected replays.
